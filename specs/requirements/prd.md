# Tiny team notes board

## Problem Statement

A team has no lightweight place to share quick, short messages with everyone. Chat scrolls away and email is heavy, so small updates get lost or never sent.

## Solution

A tiny board where a team member posts a short text note and everyone on the team sees all notes, newest first.

## Actors

- Team member: signs in, posts notes, and sees every note on the board.
